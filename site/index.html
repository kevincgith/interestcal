Rate table

Simple interest at the judgment debt rate published by the HK Judiciary, the HSBC best lending (prime) rate published by the HKMA and cross-checked daily against HSBC’s official prime rate page, or a fixed rate, with optional principal added later and partial payments. The start date earns interest and the end date does not. Interest for each period = principal × rate × days ÷ year days, where the year days depend on the day count basis you choose.

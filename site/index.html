HK Interest Calculator

Simple interest at either the judgment debt rate published by the HK Judiciary or the HSBC best lending (prime) rate published by the HKMA. The start date earns interest and the end date does not. Interest for each period = principal × rate × days ÷ year days, where the year days depend on the day count basis you choose.
